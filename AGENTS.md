# Design

**Read `DESIGN.md` in full before building or editing any page.** The homepage
is the design reference for the whole site; `DESIGN.md` codifies its tokens,
type scale, colour, layout rhythm, components, responsive rules, motion and
copy rules.

Most page briefs supply flow and copy and little or no visual direction. That
is deliberate: the brief owns what a section says, and **you own what it looks
like**. `DESIGN.md` §1 is the mandate for that — a decision procedure for
choosing each section's visual, a contradiction check, and a page-level variety
check. Run all three before writing code, not after.

New pages use the editorial system described there, not the legacy styling
still present on older routes.
